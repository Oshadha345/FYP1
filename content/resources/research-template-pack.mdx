## Template Checklist

- Claim
- Evidence
- Counterexample
- Experiment
- Decision

Weighted decision score:

$$
S = 0.5E + 0.3R + 0.2C
$$

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence | Supports the claim |
| Risk | Captures unknowns |
| Cost | Estimates implementation effort |
