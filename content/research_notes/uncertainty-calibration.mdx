> [!IMPORTANT]
> Track whether calibration is measured before or after thresholding; the interpretation changes.

Expected calibration error:

$$
\operatorname{ECE} = \sum_{m=1}^{M}\frac{|B_m|}{n}\left|\operatorname{acc}(B_m)-\operatorname{conf}(B_m)\right|
$$

| Bin | Confidence | Accuracy | Gap |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.6-0.7 | 0.64 | 0.59 | 0.05 |
| 0.7-0.8 | 0.74 | 0.73 | 0.01 |

```python
def calibration_gap(confidence: float, accuracy: float) -> float:
    return abs(confidence - accuracy)
```

See [@guo2017] for the canonical framing.
