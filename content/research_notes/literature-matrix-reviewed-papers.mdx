## Reviewed Groups

| Group | Papers / directions | Use in FYP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change detection baselines | ChangeFormer, LDGuid, ChangeFlow, DDPM-CD, consistency regularization | Establish baseline families and failure modes. |
| Department-related work | Precision fusion, Mamba-FCS, diffusion-based feature extraction, semi-supervised CD | Align with local research direction and advisor context. |
| Vision encoders | ViT, Representative Attention, VMamba, Vision Mamba, MambaVision, SF-Mamba, Spatial-Mamba | Select encoder candidates for controlled benchmarking. |
| Segmentation and open-vocabulary RS | Frequency-conditioned spatial adaptation, open-vocabulary RS segmentation | Inform boundary handling and dataset bias analysis. |
| Sequence/state-space foundations | S4, Mamba, Mamba-3, InfoMamba, DyG-Mamba, Block-Biased Mamba | Understand why SSMs may be useful for long-range spatial context. |

## Working Decisions

| Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Start with controlled binary change detection benchmarks. | It gives a clean evaluation surface before proposing a new model. |
| Track CNN, Transformer, and SSM/VSS encoders separately. | Their strengths and costs are different. |
| Record dataset bias and class limitations from the beginning. | Disaster data can be sparse, imbalanced, and domain-shifted. |
| Keep local department work visible. | It clarifies how this FYP connects to ongoing research at Peradeniya. |
