## Findings

| Area | Observation |
| --- | --- |

| Sentinel imagery | Sentinel satellites provide useful resolution and revisit characteristics for disaster monitoring. |

## Decisions

| Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Use Google Earth Engine for early data exploration. | It reduces data acquisition friction and supports reproducible search. |
| Start with Sentinel imagery for disaster planning. | It is accessible and suitable for regional change monitoring. |
| Record dataset assumptions explicitly. | Annotation quality and source differences can affect benchmark conclusions. |
