## Public Loss Summary

The public summary keeps the exact loss composition without exposing extra implementation details:

$$
L = L_{BCE} + L_{Dice} + 0.4L_{coarse} + 0.1L_{boundary}
$$

BCE and Dice supervise the final prediction. The coarse loss stabilizes the region decoder. The boundary term encourages contour consistency.
