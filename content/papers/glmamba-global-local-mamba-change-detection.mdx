## Reading Note

GLMamba is relevant to Week 05 because it studies efficient global-local feature modeling for bitemporal remote sensing change detection. I am treating it as a literature reference for comparing state-space style encoders, not as a disclosure of our own design.

## Useful Questions

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| How are global and local cues balanced? | Change detection needs both large-scene context and small boundary details. |
| How is bitemporal information fused? | Fusion design affects false positives and missed changes. |
| What is the efficiency tradeoff? | FYP work needs to compare accuracy with deployability. |

## Public Takeaway

State-space models are being actively explored in remote sensing change detection because they provide long-range modeling without the same cost profile as full self-attention.
