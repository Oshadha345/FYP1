## Core Reading Note

LDGuid argues that a model should not be forced to discover semantic difference implicitly. It pretrains a difference embedding module and uses that latent difference as guidance for the main change detection model.

## FYP Relevance

| Point | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Explicit difference modeling | Supports the idea of separating change evidence from background variation. |
| Bottleneck training | Connects to representation compression and task-relevant feature extraction. |
| Robustness | Relevant for disaster data where spectral noise and domain shift are expected. |
