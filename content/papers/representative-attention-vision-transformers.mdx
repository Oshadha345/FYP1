## Core Reading Note

The main idea is to gather spatial tokens into learned representative tokens, let those representatives interact globally, then distribute information back to the original tokens.

## FYP Relevance

| Point | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Efficient global context | Relevant when attention cost becomes expensive at high resolution. |
| Semantic token grouping | May be useful when changed regions are spatially separated but semantically related. |
| Benchmark role | It is a Transformer-efficiency direction, not a direct change detection model. |
