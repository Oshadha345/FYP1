## Core Reading Note

ChangeFormer is a key early paper for understanding why Transformer-based encoders became important in remote sensing change detection. The central idea is to use Siamese feature extraction with stronger global context modeling than a purely convolutional design.

| Why compare encoders? | Encoder choice changes the type of spatial and temporal evidence the model can use. |
