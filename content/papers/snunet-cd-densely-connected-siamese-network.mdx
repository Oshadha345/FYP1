## Reading Note

SNUNet-CD is a CNN-family change detection baseline. It is useful for understanding why dense encoder-decoder connections and shallow localization information matter in VHR change detection.

## FYP Relevance

| Point | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| CNN comparison | Helps compare CNN-based change detection against Transformer and SSM backbones. |
| Boundary concern | Dense skip connections are relevant when studying small object and boundary preservation. |
| Week 3 context | It supports the controlled benchmark design stage. |
