Bayes' rule:

$$
p(\theta \mid D) = \frac{p(D \mid \theta)p(\theta)}{p(D)}
$$

| Term | Role |
| --- | --- |
| $p(\theta)$ | Prior belief |
| $p(D \mid \theta)$ | Likelihood |
| $p(\theta \mid D)$ | Posterior belief |

```r
posterior <- likelihood * prior / evidence
```
